Jingtong (Sophia) He

B.S. Biochemistry, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign · Expected Dec 2026

I am a senior at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign studying Biochemistry, with minors in Chemistry and Theatre. In the Gritton Lab, I study how hippocampal and striatal circuits support learning. My work combines in vivo calcium imaging, LFP recording, and behavior with quantitative analysis of neural population dynamics.

My senior thesis examines how parvalbumin-positive (PV+) interneurons in hippocampal CA1 reorganize during the extinction of trace eyeblink conditioning. For that project I handled everything from stereotaxic surgery and cranial window implantation to building Python pipelines for population and time-frequency analysis.

I am pursuing graduate study in Biomedical Engineering, where I want to develop imaging and computational tools for understanding the brain and translating those methods into biomedical applications.
